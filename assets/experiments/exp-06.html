HOW TO PLAY

  • 1. Hold SPACEBAR or tap LAUNCH to pull back plunger and shoot ball.
  • 2. Use A / Left Arrow for Left Flipper, D / Right Arrow for Right Flipper.
  • 3. Bounce off circular bumpers (+100 pts) and triangular slingshots (+50 pts).
  • 4. Light up all top lane indicators to increase score multiplier up to 5X!
